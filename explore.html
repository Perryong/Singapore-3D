THE SMALL PRINT

A map for exploring.

Food places come from NEA and OpenStreetMap. This includes mapped hawker centres, restaurants, cafés and food outlets, but not every individual stall. The Vegetarian filter includes venues explicitly tagged fully vegetarian or vegan in OpenStreetMap. These are community-reported dietary labels, not independently certified; listings and menus may change.

MRT stations and exits come from LTA DataMall; rail geometry comes directly from OpenStreetMap. Station markers are derived from building footprints. Stations are matched to LTA’s code list, so coverage may lag newer OSM routes. Zoom in to see entrances. This is not live train or service information.

Nearby distances are straight-line distances, not walking routes. Recorded opening hours may have changed.
